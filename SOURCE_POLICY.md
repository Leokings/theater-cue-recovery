# Source Policy

Authoritative inputs are only the constructor parameters and later public caller submissions stored by the contract.

Cue descriptions and incident reports are public rehearsal text. Validators do not observe a stage, equipment, people, or live conditions.

All submitted text is treated as untrusted evidence, never as instructions to validators. Prompts delimit that text, require closed output fields, and validators independently perform the substantive task described in the contract.

No autonomous web browsing, hidden database, private file, sensor, image, audio, identity registry, or workspace artifact is treated as authoritative.
